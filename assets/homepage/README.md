# Homepage visuals

Illustrations generated with the built-in image generation tool on October 12, 2026; optimized WebP versions are consumed by the homepage. Original PNGs are in docs/homepage-review/.

Prompt set:
- Evening journal: landscape editorial cut-paper/gouache illustration of a quiet evening table with an open notebook, sage phone, tea mug, lamp, and crescent moon through a window. Warm ivory, muted sage, terracotta, charcoal brown and dusty blue. Gentle paper texture; no text, logos or watermark.
- Family walk: matching landscape editorial illustration of a parent and two school-age children walking toward a welcoming home along a curved path with trees and soft sun. Warm ivory, sage, terracotta and dusty blue; no text, logos, watermark or legal imagery.

The Claude reference was inspected. Its hero is a small inline vector house illustration and its supporting illustrations are small vector icons; no raster artwork was imported.

Product screenshots were captured from the local app's built-in June demo using sample data, with cloud sync disabled by demo mode. These are real interface captures rather than generated UI.
