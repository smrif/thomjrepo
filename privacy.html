Custody Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Alpha Privacy Summary

During this alpha, Custody Tracker does not have user accounts or cloud sync. Your custody records are stored in your browser on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Information Stored in the App

The app may store the information you enter, including your name or label, co-parent label, email address, children's names, custody entries, notes, timestamps, and attached screenshot data.

Where Your Data Lives

In this alpha version, app data is stored locally in your browser storage. We do not currently run a backend account system for syncing or backing up your custody records.

Exports and Sharing

If you export a CSV, email a report, print a report, or submit feedback, you control that action. Exported files and shared reports may contain sensitive custody information, so store and send them carefully.

Feedback Form

The feedback button may open a Google Form. If you submit that form, your response is handled by Google and may be visible to the Custody Tracker team for product feedback. Avoid including sensitive legal details unless you are comfortable sharing them through that form.

Data Loss Risk

Because this alpha stores records locally, clearing browser data, changing devices, uninstalling the browser, or using private browsing may delete your records. Export regularly if you want a backup.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your custody records.
  • We do not intentionally share your local app data with other parents, attorneys, or courts.
  • We do not currently provide cloud backup, cross-device sync, or account recovery.

Future Changes

If cloud accounts, payments, analytics, or attorney-sharing features are added later, this policy should be updated before those features are used by testers.
