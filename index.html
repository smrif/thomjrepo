HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. A few minutes a day.

01

Check in before bed

Record who had the kids, what was scheduled, and anything that changed. Add a note or screenshot for context.

02

See the picture build

Your entries become a dated calendar and diary. Today and yesterday stay editable; older entries become read-only in the app.

03

Ready when you need it

Create a dated report to print or share with an attorney, a mediator, or just yourself. Download a CSV backup of your records, too.

How counts and exports work

Counts cover the selected period. Custody and schedule changes count family dates, including split days, rather than children or hours. Pressure is user-reported on schedule-change dates. Screenshots counts records with an attachment.

Missing records have unknown custody. Reports reference screenshots without including image files. CSV includes attachment details; it is not a restorable backup or a package of screenshot files.
